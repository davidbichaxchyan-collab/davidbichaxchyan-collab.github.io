Privacy policy

Inbox Reminder is a private tool used only by its owner on their own accounts. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

What it reads

With the Gmail permission "View your email message metadata" it reads only email headers and labels: sender, recipients, subject and dates. It cannot read the text of emails or attachments. It never sends, deletes or changes anything in the mailbox.

What it keeps, and where

  • A copy of the last 30 days' email headers, to see which conversations wait for a reply. Older items are deleted.
  • The addresses the owner has written to in the last year, to mark new senders.
  • The owner's own choices in the tool (marked important, muted), until changed.

This data is stored only on the computer or server that runs the tool. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Where it goes

The daily reminder, with sender names and subjects, is sent to the owner's own Telegram account.

Google API data

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have the stored data deleted, write to the contact below.
